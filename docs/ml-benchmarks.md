## Clustering

Every row is scored from its returned centroids the same way: MSE is the mean squared distance from each point to its nearest centroid (train, and held-out test points); silhouette is on a 2,000-point sample of the training set; dead clusters own fewer than ten training points. Distances counts point-to-centroid distance evaluations (scratch implementations count exactly; FAISS rows are exact by construction; scikit-learn does not expose it). Epochs are passes over the training data. Lloyd and Elkan stop when no label changes (at most 100 iterations); faiss.Kmeans runs its fixed 25 iterations; mini-batch and e-means get 3 epochs; both EM implementations stop when the mean log-likelihood gains less than 1e-3.
