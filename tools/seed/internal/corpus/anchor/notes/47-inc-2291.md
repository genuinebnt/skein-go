Severity 2. Date 2026-08-19. Duration 47 minutes of degraded writes, 11 minutes of no writes. Blameless.

## Summary

A backfill job tripled write volume. Compaction could not keep up, the L0 file count reached 36, and the storage engine stopped accepting writes until compaction caught up.

## Root cause

The engine ran with max_background_compactions = 2, sized for normal load. With the backfill, L0 grew faster than two threads could merge it, and the write stall threshold of 36 L0 files was reached.

## Mitigation

Raised max_background_compactions from 2 to 6 at 14:38 UTC. Writes resumed at 14:49 UTC.

## Action items

- [x] Alert when L0 exceeds 20 files
- [x] Keep 6 compaction threads permanently
- [ ] Rate-limit backfills to 50 MB/s
- [ ] Load test with 3x write volume before each release
