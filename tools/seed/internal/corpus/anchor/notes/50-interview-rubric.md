Four interviews, each scored 1 to 4 against written signals. Decisions are made from the scores and notes, never from a debrief vote.

| Interview | Strong signal | Weak signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Systems design | Names the failure modes before being asked | Draws boxes without data flow |
| Coding | Tests their own code unprompted | Debugs by guessing |
| Storage deep dive | Explains a real tradeoff they made | Recites definitions |
| Collaboration | Describes disagreeing well | Has never disagreed |

A 1 in any interview is a no unless the interviewer writes why it should not be.
