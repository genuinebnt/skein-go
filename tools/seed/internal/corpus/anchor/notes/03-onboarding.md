Copy this list into your own note on day one and tick items as you go. Ask in #kestrel-help when something is out of date.

## Week one

- [x] Get a laptop and enrol it in device management
- [x] Read [[Kestrel Lab Home]] and [[Glossary]]
- [x] Run the local stack with `make up`
- [ ] Pair with the on-call engineer for one shift
- [ ] Ship one small change to production

## Week two

- [ ] Read [[Raft overview]] and [[LSM tree overview]]
- [ ] Review two pull requests outside your team
- [ ] Present one thing you learned at Friday demos

> The checklist is a floor, not a ceiling. Nobody will notice if you skip "Present one thing", but you will.
